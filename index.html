Terms & Disclaimer

Fine Booking is a platform that connects guests with hotels, motels, guesthouses, Airbnbs and restaurants across Uganda. Here's how it works, and what you should know.

What we do

We help guests discover places to stay and eat, and we pass booking requests along to hosts. Where a listing has its own booking or payment system, we connect you directly to it.

What we don't do

Fine Booking does not process payments, and we are not a party to the booking agreement between you and the host. Payment and final arrangements are handled directly between the guest and the host.

No guarantees

While we do our best to list genuine, verified hosts, Fine Booking cannot guarantee the accuracy of listings, availability, pricing, or property condition. Please confirm details directly with the host before paying.

Your responsibility

Any dispute, damage, loss or disagreement arising from a booking is between you and the host directly. Fine Booking is not liable for issues arising from bookings made through the platform.

Fix listing locations (one-time)

Looks up every listing's real suburb/town from its coordinates and corrects the saved location text — fixes places that got mislabeled with whatever area name was typed into a bulk search (e.g. a place actually in Katabi but tagged "Entebbe"). Safe to run more than once; it skips anything already correct.

Bulk import places (from OpenStreetMap)

Pull in real places by name and location only — no photos or prices, since that data isn't ours to copy. These land as "quick add" listings you can edit later as you contact each one.

Airbnbs aren't included — short-term rentals aren't tagged distinctly in this open map data, so those still need to be added one by one.

A wide radius (10–20km) can take a minute or two — each place without existing address data gets individually checked so it's labeled correctly, rather than lumped under the area you typed.
